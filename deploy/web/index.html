    Bug bounties & VDPs

    Marketplaces are aggregators you join to pick programs (HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Immunefi…). Programs are a single organization's own bounty or disclosure channel (MSRC, Apple, CERTs/NCSCs…). Contracts are the individual program listings hosted inside marketplaces — live on the Contracts tab, refreshed on a schedule. Search by company, asset, or vuln class; blank lists all.
